# HakuX for RG477M — 1.2.0-rg477m.2

[Русский](1.2.0-rg477m.2.ru.md) | [English](1.2.0-rg477m.2.en.md)

Prepared and installed on RG477M on 2026-10-28. Based on hakuX 1.2.0, versionCode 10, Android arm64-v8a, release/R8, debuggable=false.

## New in this build

- Official MaliSX2 0.2.3 is bundled in the APK, with its SHA-256, notices and licenses. Manual driver download/import is no longer needed on a fresh supported installation.
- Device detection includes RG477M and Dimensity 8300/8300 Ultra (MT6897), Android API 29+, arm64 and `/dev/mali0`. The tested hardware is RG477M / MT6897 / Mali-G615 MC6 / Android 14; detection alone does not establish compatibility on every other MT6897 device.
- A fresh installation selects the built-in driver automatically. An identical previously imported MaliSX2 binary migrates once. Existing explicit system/other custom driver choices are preserved. The built-in package remains available in the driver list and cannot be deleted there.
- The fresh-install frame limit defaults to 120 FPS; existing saved values are retained. The supplied icon, single-line FPS/frame-time HUD and all earlier RG477M workarounds are retained.
- Update checks now use `Wannamingo/hakuX-for-RG477M`. Numeric fork revisions are compared correctly. APKs must match the installed package ID and signing certificate. Android still asks before installing an update.
- The actual settings switch was exercised in both directions: 16:9 produces a 1280×720 viewport at (0,120) on the 1280×960 display; 4:3 produces 1280×960 at (0,0). The setting takes effect on the next game start; per-game overrides take precedence. The original 4:3 preference was restored.

## Retained adaptation changes

- MaliSX2 import/loading on RG477M, actual driver verification and explicit load errors.
- A bounded 64-fence completion queue and compatible presentation for drivers without timeline semaphores.
- Syberia and Ultimate Beach Soccer: fixed early exit from file-backed images by handling the in-memory XBE media flags.
- Serious Sam: guest SEH delivery and REP MOVS progress recovery fix the startup crash.
- Ultimate Spider-Man: PAGE_WRITECOMBINE attributes are preserved using the expanded upstream 1.2.0 memory fix, including MmSetAddressProtect and compatible MM v2 state fields.
- Serious Sam: unavailable/incomplete visibility queries no longer become false zero results, fixing black flicker and severe input delay.
- Warrior Within: linear video textures are matched by pitch, texel size and dimensions, fixing stripes and the stretched lower half of cutscenes.
- The Two Thrones: unaligned vertex arrays are repacked without changing attribute values, fixing excess character geometry and triangles.
- The Sands of Time: exact bloom texture bounds remove the green right edge while retaining the effect.
- The Sands of Time / MaliSX2 0.2.3: splitting legacy render passes at blend/write-mask transitions and synchronizing attachments remove flickering black sky/floor blocks.
- FPS limits: unlimited, 30, 60, 90 or 120. The tested console retains 120 FPS; the limiter avoids a frame backlog and preserves a game’s own lower cap.
- The release HUD has one row for FPS and average/last frame time. Lower diagnostic rows and temporary experimental modes are absent.
- The supplied artwork is used for legacy, adaptive and monochrome icons across five densities, preserving its aspect ratio.
- Updated to hakuX 1.2.0, retaining upstream audio, memory, thread-stack, controller and System Link changes. present_cpu_wait and rop_pass_split have distinct bits; fpslimit uses the shared parser, and the adrenotools hooks are built together.

## Validation

Native Release and optimized Android release builds passed, including lintVitalRelease and all 24 JVM tests. A disposable clean-install app verified automatic driver selection, exact library/archive checksums, and preservation of a system-driver choice; the temporary app was removed. Main-package in-place installation, APK identity, v3 signing and 16 KiB alignment were verified. Device logs confirm the bundled MaliSX2 library, ordered fences, 120 FPS limit, both aspect ratios and the fork update feed. The feed returned .1 and correctly did not offer a downgrade from .2. Download/install of a future newer fork release cannot be tested until such a release exists.
